## Current (reads a little odd — four ideas crammed together, plus jargon)
> I run all four disciplines as one engine, wired together by a measurement spine.
> I have spent thirteen years across brand, product, community and growth, because
> the leaks do not respect the org chart. Fan-led work is usually felt, not counted.
> I tie every part to a number, so what you build is something you can put in front
> of a board.

Problems: "measurement spine" is jargon; "the leaks do not respect the org chart"
is cryptic; the sentence juggles the engine, the bio, the metaphor and the
measurement point at once.

Keepers: the four-run-as-one thesis; the "felt, not counted → tied to a number →
board-ready" idea (strong differentiator); the cross-functional credibility.
