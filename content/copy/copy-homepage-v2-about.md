# Options D and E (current — written after the region confirmation)

## What the earlier options lost

The live homepage earns its authority with three beats that Cowork's version
does not have:

1. **"Complicated tech, crowded roadmaps, budgets from shoestring to
   enormous."** Specific and vivid. Shows range without listing more logos.
2. **"I've worked the whole machine."** The strongest line in the whole
   section. It says she has seen it from every seat, which is a different and
   better claim than having worked at good companies.
3. **"Which is how I know where growth really comes from, and where it
   leaks."** The bridge that turns the CV into a reason to trust her.

Beat 2 is the one worth the words. A roster tells you where she has been;
"I've worked the whole machine" tells you what she can see. Options D and E
both rescue it.

## Option D — recommended

> Thirteen years building brand, community and growth where fans are loudest,
> for global brands and startups across North America and EMEA: Ubisoft,
> Amazon Games and BlaBlaCar, then VP Marketing of a US startup acquired by
> Animoca. I've worked the whole machine.
>
> Now I run my own consultancy for fan-led growth, bringing what worked in
> those rooms together with a method of my own: the **Fan Engine™**. Your fans
> do the selling. I can prove the return.

Cowork's sequencing intact: past → now → two-beat close. Adds four words to
recover the best line from the live site. The paragraph now ends on a claim
rather than trailing off on a job title, which also gives paragraph one its
own small punch to mirror paragraph two's.

Reads as: here is where I have been, here is what that means, here is what I
do now, here is what you get.

## Option E — fuller, if you want the texture back

> Thirteen years building brand, community and growth where fans are loudest,
> for global brands and startups across North America and EMEA: Ubisoft,
> Amazon Games and BlaBlaCar, then VP Marketing of a US startup acquired by
> Animoca. Complicated tech, crowded roadmaps, budgets from shoestring to
> enormous. I've worked the whole machine.
>
> Now I run my own consultancy for fan-led growth, bringing what worked in
> those rooms together with a method of my own: the **Fan Engine™**. Your fans
> do the selling. I can prove the return.

Recovers beats 1 and 2. The "complicated tech, crowded roadmaps" line is the
most human sentence in the section and the only place the copy admits the work
is hard.

**Trade-off:** paragraph one goes to four sentences. This section was cut back
because it read heavy, so this is the option most likely to undo that. Worth
trying on screen before deciding, since it reads shorter than it looks.

## Not recommended: adding beat 3 as well

Bringing back "which is how I know where growth really comes from, and where
it leaks" on top of D or E makes paragraph one do too much, and it partly
duplicates "I've worked the whole machine". Pick one authority line, not two.

## Note on what was lost

The old second paragraph carried the argument "the biggest opportunity most
companies walk past is the customers they already have." That thought is now
gone from this section. It is not lost from the page: the hero lede opens with
"the customers you already paid for are worth far more than you're getting,"
which is the same idea in the reader's own terms and lands earlier.
